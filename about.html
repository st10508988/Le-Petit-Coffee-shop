About Le Petite Coffee

Discover more about our coffee shop and what we offer.

Our Story

Le Petite Coffee is a small coffee shop that’s located in Oakdene, Johannesburg South.

The café serves freshly brewed coffee, teas, hot chocolate, a variety of pastries, sandwiches, and light meals in a welcoming environment.

It mainly relies on walk-in customers and social media, such as Instagram (@le.petite.coffee), to promote their business.

Our Mission

The main mission for the café is to provide high-quality coffee, delicious food, and excellent customer service in a cosy and welcoming setting.

Our Vision

Their vision is to establish an online presence and provide exceptional client experiences to become one of Johannesburg South’s most favoured coffee shops.

Our Customers

Students, working professionals, families, visitors, and locals searching for good coffee, food, and a cosy space to connect or work are among the target demographic.
